What cybersecurity skills will I learn from these courses?

Our courses cover essential cybersecurity skills including penetration testing, web application security, network security, Active Directory exploitation, and malware analysis. Each course is designed to provide hands-on, practical knowledge that you can apply immediately in real-world scenarios.

Are these courses suitable for beginners?

Yes! We offer courses at various skill levels. Some courses are designed for complete beginners, while others are intermediate to advanced. Each course description clearly indicates the required skill level and prerequisites.

Do I need special equipment or software?

Most courses can be completed with a standard computer and internet connection. We'll provide guidance on any free tools or software you'll need. For hands-on labs, we'll guide you through setting up safe testing environments.

Are these courses really free?

Yes, all our current courses are completely free! We believe cybersecurity education should be accessible to everyone. We may offer premium courses in the future, but we'll always maintain a selection of free courses.
